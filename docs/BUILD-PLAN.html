POPIA — Data Residency

All personal data stays in South Africa (Vultr JHB). Only Resend is cross-border — covered by POPIA s.72(1)(c) (necessary for contract performance) + signed DPA.

POPIA Obligations (regardless of hosting)

  • Privacy Policy disclosing every third-party processor and its hosting region
  • Cookie banner with granular consent (Necessary / Analytics / Marketing)
  • data_requests table + /store/data-requests endpoint (Phase 5)
  • 36-hour breach notification process — Information Regulator + affected subjects
  • Information Officer named and registered with the Information Regulator (client's MD by default)
  • Signed DPAs with Resend, Meta, Anthropic, Cloudflare before go-live
  • Retention schedule: orders 5 years (SARS), marketing consent re-confirmed annually, abandoned-cart data purged after 90 days
