Y02 — Decision one

Which address should be your main one?

We need your answer

tse.co.za — the name your customers already know, registered since 1997, carrying nearly three decades of search history and links.

tse-cartridges.co.za — where the new store currently lives. Registered May 2026, and the hyphen is easily mistyped or lost over the phone.

The deciding evidence: search for printer cartridges today and tse.co.za comes up on the first page. tse-cartridges.co.za does not appear at all — it is four months old and has not yet earned a position.

Our recommendation Keep tse.co.za as the main address and run the new store there. It's the stronger asset, it's the one already on your invoices, signage and email, and 29 years of Google standing is not something we can rebuild.

Both options work technically — the redirect map handles either. They are not equal in cost, though: pointing tse.co.za at the new site keeps what it has earned, while retiring it starts from zero on a new name.

One trade-off you should know about. Your Google Shopping listings are approved against tse-cartridges.co.za. Moving to tse.co.za means re-verifying with Google, and the listings may be off for a few days while that clears. We'd schedule it for a quiet week — but it is a real cost, and it's the reason to decide once rather than change our minds later.

Y07 — What we recommend

Four things, in the order we'd do them

  • 01

    Reorder and review emails

    Cartridges run out on a predictable cycle, and you have a base of past buyers — but nothing currently goes out to them. This needs no extra visitors to the site and is the fastest money on the table. You also have no product reviews at all, which is free credibility going unclaimed.

  • 02

    Serve the business buyer properly

    Bulk pricing, quote requests, purchase orders and quick reordering — built around how your actual customers buy.

  • 03

    Build out the printer pages

    Customers search for their printer model, not the cartridge code. The new site already has a page for around 750 printer models — filling those out properly is the biggest untapped source of new visitors you have.

  • 04

    Local search

    You deliver same-day in Johannesburg and Pretoria. Your Google Business Profile and some delivery-area pages should be working much harder for you.
