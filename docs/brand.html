TSE Online — Brand Guidelines

TriNext Innovations · Confirmed 2026-05-15 · brand.html

1. Brand Identity

Technical Systems Engineering (TSE) has supplied quality generic/compatible printer cartridges since 1987, from Unit 34, A.P.D. Industrial Park, Kya Sands, Johannesburg.

TSE sells only generic/compatible cartridges — never OEM originals. Value proposition: same print quality as OEM at a fraction of the price, backed by a replacement guarantee.

The logo's halftone dot gradient — lime, cyan, pink, black — represents the four CMYK inks. TSE is literally a colour company.

  • Clear space: minimum half the logo height on all sides

5. Brand Voice

Tone

  • Direct and confident — no filler words, no corporate hedging
  • Honest — generic is the product; own it as the selling point
  • Practical — SA offices care about price, reliability, and speed

Core Lines (use verbatim)

  • "Generic. Not generic." — hero headline
  • "Works as good, or even better than the original." — the guarantee
  • "Order before noon, on your desk tomorrow." — delivery promise (JHB/PTA)
  • "South Africa's printer-cartridge specialist since 1987." — trust anchor

6. Imagery

  • Clean product shots on white or near-white backgrounds
  • Current source: WooCommerce media library export — sufficient for relaunch
  • Avoid: lifestyle shots that obscure the product; generic stock office imagery
  • Client to supply updated photography post-launch (issue #25)

7. Iconography

  • SVG icons only — no emoji as UI icons
  • Preferred: Lucide or Heroicons — consistent 24×24 viewBox, strokeWidth="2"
  • Minimum touch target: 44×44px
  • Icon colour: currentColor — never hardcode fill

9. Layout & Spacing

  • Max content width: max-w-7xl (80rem / 1280px)
  • Page horizontal padding: px-4 sm:px-8 lg:px-12
  • Section vertical padding: py-16 sm:py-24 (standard) / py-20 sm:py-28 (feature)
  • Border radius: rounded-[20px] product cards · rounded-[24px] bento cards · rounded-[28px] CTA · rounded-full pills
  • Z-index: z-10 content · z-20 overlays · z-40 nav · z-50 modals
